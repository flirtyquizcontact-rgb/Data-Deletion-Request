SeeU: separate account and GPS deletion process

SeeU is different from the offline apps listed above. SeeU creates a Google/Firebase account and may store profile, Circle, location history, shared location, messages and safety data in Firebase. The statements below about apps without accounts or cloud data do not apply to SeeU.

To request SeeU account and associated data deletion, use the dedicated SeeU account deletion page or email quizfans.studio@gmail.com. The deletion workflow is still under development and is not yet verified end to end.

Summary

The applications listed above are designed to work primarily offline and do not require user accounts. We do not collect, store, sell, or upload personal user files to our own servers. Most data remains locally on the user’s device.

What data is collected

  • No user accounts: The mainly offline apps listed above do not require account creation or login. SeeU is an exception and has a separate deletion notice.
  • No cloud file storage: Files transferred using our apps remain on the user’s device or are sent directly to another device chosen by the user.
  • No personal files uploaded to our servers: We do not upload user-selected photos, videos, documents, or other files to our own servers.
  • Technical data via Google services: Some apps may use Google Firebase, Crashlytics, Analytics, and Google AdMob. These services may process technical data such as device information, app version, operating system version, crash logs, diagnostics, app interactions, advertising data, device identifiers, and approximate location derived from IP address, according to Google’s policies.

Data that can be deleted

  • Support or contact requests: If we hold any support message, contact email, or deletion request related to your use of our apps, we can delete it upon request.
  • Locally stored app data: You can delete local app data directly from your Android device by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage from Android settings.

Data not stored by us

  • Transferred files: Files transferred inside our apps are stored on the user’s own device or sent directly to another device chosen by the user. We do not have remote access to these files and cannot delete them remotely.
  • Local files: Photos, videos, documents, and other files stored on your device must be deleted by you directly from your device.

DirectBeam Windows Companion: remove pairing and local PC data

DirectBeam Windows Companion is an optional desktop app paired with DirectBeam on Android. It does not require a separate DirectBeam account. The files you transfer are saved on the devices you choose; we cannot remotely erase copies stored on your PC.

  1. Disconnect or unpair the phone in Windows Companion and revoke the corresponding PC connection on Android where the feature is available.
  2. Delete downloaded or exported files from their Windows destination (for example, Downloads\DirectBeam), including any other copies you made.
  3. To clear locally stored pairing metadata and pairing diagnostics, inspect and remove the appropriate files under %LOCALAPPDATA%\DirectBeam\Companion\, including phone.json and pairing-diagnostic.log where present.
  4. Remove DirectBeam Companion pairing credentials for the device from Windows Credential Manager if the app's unpair action has not already removed them. Do not delete unrelated Windows credentials.
  5. If you uninstall Windows Companion, check these locations separately: uninstallation does not necessarily erase downloaded files, pairing metadata, logs or stored credentials.

For more information about local PC processing, read the DirectBeam Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. For information you actually sent to our support mailbox, contact quizfans.studio@gmail.com with a deletion request. Do not attach private pairing codes or transfer-file contents unless specifically needed.

Data retained and retention period

  • Google services data: Technical data processed by Google Firebase, Crashlytics, Analytics, or AdMob is handled according to Google’s own data retention and deletion policies.
  • Support emails and deletion requests: We may retain support or deletion request emails for up to 90 days for security, compliance, and record-keeping purposes, unless a longer period is legally required.

What happens after a request

  • We review the deletion request.
  • If we hold any data that can be deleted by us, we delete it.
  • If the data is stored only on the user’s device or processed by Google services, we explain what can be deleted locally or through the relevant service provider.
  • We aim to respond to valid requests within a reasonable timeframe.
